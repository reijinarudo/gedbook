Samuel Hollis was born in Lowndes County, Alabama, in 1895, the son of Isaac Hollis, a tenant farmer. The 1900 and 1910 censuses place him on his father's rented farm in Beat 5.

In June 1917 he registered for the World War I draft. The card describes him as tall and of medium build and names his father as his employer.

By 1930 he had moved his family to Montgomery, where he owned his home and worked at a lumber yard.
